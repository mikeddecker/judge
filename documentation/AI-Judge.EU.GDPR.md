# Why?

Concerning any European citizen, even if a non-European party wants to process personal data.
Protects the EU inhabitants of unwanted or uninformed use of their personal data.
Personal data includes:
	- Anything which can identify a person -> video's included
	- Includes 2FA to accounts where personal data is stored

# GDPR & Privacy Notice
GDPR will enlist al lawfully required things needed to take into account. 
The privacy notice will explain which data is used, stored and processed.

- A privacy notice explains **how and why** you process personal data.
- The law obligates organisations to inform people transparently.
- Permission is only one of the possible legal bases.

🇽 “Agreeing to a privacy statement” is usually legally meaningless
✅ “I give consent for X” must be specific, free and active
